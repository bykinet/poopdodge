# Ending Scenarios and Generation Prompts

## Success Ending (성공 엔딩)

### 3-Second Scenario
- **0.0s**: The final shadow misses by a fraction, and the hero looks up toward the clear sky.
- **1.0s**: The giant butt has vanished completely; only distant birds fly peacefully across the blue sky.
- **2.0s**: The hero performs a joyful light hop; warm sunlight illuminates the cozy anime alley.
- **3.0s**: The hero's relieved, triumphant smile freezes into a victorious still pose.
- **Audio**: Joyful cheer arpeggio, fading cleanly when transitioning to the still frame.


## Failure Ending (실패 엔딩)

### 3-Second Scenario
- **0.0s**: A falling poop splats right next to the hero's feet; the hero flinches in comedic shock.
- **1.0s**: The hero's shoulders slump heavily as the alley light subtly dims into gloomy twilight.
- **2.0s**: The giant butt retreats up into the clouds leaving a harmless cartoon splat on the ground.
- **3.0s**: The hero freezes in a hilarious, dejected slumped posture with little anime defeat tears.
- **Audio**: Comedic dejected sigh, ending cleanly upon reaching the still frame.

### Failure Still Prompt
```text
Cel-shaded Japanese anime comedy illustration, full-body standing character centered in a dim twilight Japanese alleyway, dejected slumped posture with comical teary eyes, a harmless stylized cartoon splat mark on the cobblestone ground in front, empty open sky above, 16:9 widescreen composition, cute comedy anime style, vibrant harmonious palette, absolutely no text, no typography, no watermarks, no photorealism.
```

### Failure Scene Animation (GIF) Prompt
```text
3.0 second animation sequence in cel-shaded anime style, 16:9 aspect ratio: comic hazard lands with a cartoon splat by hero's shoes, hero's shoulders droop, alley dims slightly, hero slumps in funny dejection with cartoon anime defeat expression, no text, no watermarks.
```
